What is a Clicker Fidget?

A clicker fidget is a small handheld fidget made with mechanical keyboard switches. Pressing the keys gives you the satisfying click and feel of a mechanical keyboard in a compact design you can take anywhere.

Choose different switches, keycaps, sizes, and light-up options to create a clicker that fits your style.

✓ Mechanical switches
✓ Custom keycaps
✓ 1–4 key designs
✓ Small & portable
