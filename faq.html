Clickers & customization

What is a clicker?

A clicker is a small mechanical-switch fidget with keycaps you can press. Choose from 1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, and 9-key designs in the shop.

Do they come prebuilt?

Yes! Your clickers arrive assembled with the switches and keycaps you selected, so they’re ready to click right out of the package.

What is the difference between Clicky and Creamy?

Clicky offers a crisp, tactile click. Creamy has a smooth feel and a softer sound. You can choose the switch type when customizing your clicker.

Can I choose a different keycap for every key?

Yes. Click a keycap slot on the product page to open the image picker. Choose a design for every key, then add your clicker to the cart. You can repeat a design when enough stock is available.

What can I customize on the light-up clicker?

Choose a Clicky or Creamy switch, a keycap design, and one of five light colors: White, Red, Blue, Yellow, or Green.

Bundles & stock

Can I customize the clickers in a bundle?

Yes. On each bundle page, choose Clicky or Creamy switches for each included clicker and select a keycap for every key. The image picker shows the available designs.

Why is a keycap unavailable?

The design may be out of stock, already used by items in your cart, or used by other selections in your bundle. Repeated designs each need a separate keycap. Stock is checked again before items are added.

Does adding an item to my cart reserve it?

Adding an item to your cart does not reserve stock. Inventory is checked again at checkout, so availability can change while you shop.

What if inventory cannot be checked?

Adding items is blocked until inventory can be checked. Refresh the page and try again. Your existing cart stays saved in your browser.

Shipping & checkout

Where do you ship?

Checkout currently accepts shipping addresses in the United States. Orders ship from San Marino, California, using USPS Ground Advantage.

How much does shipping cost?

Enter your US shipping address in the cart and calculate shipping to see the USPS rate before checkout. If you change the address or the quote expires, calculate shipping again.

How does checkout work?

Customize your items, open the cart, calculate shipping, select the rate, and continue to Stripe Checkout. Your payment is handled on Stripe’s secure checkout page.

What if checkout does not start?

Check that every keycap is selected and that you have a current shipping quote. If stock changed, update your selections or quantity. Your cart is kept if checkout cannot start.
