Meet ClickerLabStore

Small clickers.
Your own style.

ClickerLabStore is all about making a simple click your own. Our mechanical-switch clickers combine the feel of a keyboard switch with playful keycap designs you can mix and match.

Choose a compact single-key clicker or go bigger with a multi-key design. Pick your switches, choose a keycap for every key, and create a combination that feels like you.

Find your feel

Choose Clicky for a crisp, tactile click or Creamy for a smooth feel and a softer sound. The 1-key light-up clicker also lets you choose from five light colors.

Make every key yours

Explore keycap designs in the image picker and choose one for each key. Our standard clickers come in 1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, and 9-key sizes.

Build a little collection

Bundles bring different clicker sizes together. Customize the switches and keycaps on every included clicker, all at the bundle’s listed price.

Ready to click, right out of the package

Your clickers arrive prebuilt with the switches and keycaps you selected. Have a question about customization, stock, or shipping? Visit our FAQ page.
